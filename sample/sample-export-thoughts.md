## Inner Thoughts

The app renders a companion's inner thought outside and above its message bubble,
so it is not part of the message body, and the app's own export omits it entirely.
This section was added by the "Back to the Fig: Chat Export" browser extension,
which read the thoughts from the page. Everything from `## Conversation` onward is
byte-for-byte the app's own export.

Each `### <n>` heading names the 0-based index of a message in the `## Conversation`
section below, and the fenced block under it is that message's inner thought,
verbatim. Thoughts are intermittent: a companion message with no entry here had no
thought rendered, which is a fact about the conversation rather than a gap in this
export.
